Descriptions

One lineTFT Panel — synced lyrics and touch controls for your music, on a 7-inch screen.
ShortTFT Panel is open firmware that turns the Elecrow CrowPanel 7" into a lyrics screen for Roon. The words of the song scroll in time with the music next to the cover, and a touch gives play, skip, volume and the choice of zone. It talks only to your own TextFromTrack companion on your network.
LongTFT Panel is free, open-source firmware for the Elecrow CrowPanel 7" touch screen. Placed next to your hi-fi, it shows the lyrics of the song playing in Roon, line by line and in time with the music, over a blurred version of the cover; a swipe switches to the album art with the title and artist in large type. A tap brings play/pause, skip, a volume slider, mute and the zone to follow. Setup takes a minute from a phone, with two QR codes on the screen; installation is done from a web browser, and updates arrive over Wi-Fi with an automatic return to the previous version if one ever fails. The panel works with the TextFromTrack companion, which finds the lyrics on the user's own computer: no cloud, no account. MIT licence.
