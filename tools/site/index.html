Your lyrics, in time with the music

A 7-inch touch screen that shows the words of the song as it plays, with the cover, and puts play, skip, volume and zone at your fingertips.

Install in your browser Read the user guide
Synced lyricsThe current line large, the next ones gently sliding up, timed to a few hundredths of a second.
Cover viewSwipe for the album art with the title and artist in large type. Songs without lyrics show it by themselves.
Touch controlsPlay, pause, skip, a volume slider, mute, and the choice of the Roon zone to follow.
Set up from your phoneTwo QR codes on the screen: join the panel, pick your Wi-Fi. No app, no account.
Updates over the airA tap installs a new version; if it ever fails to start, the panel goes back by itself.
Private and openTalks only to your own TextFromTrack companion on your network. MIT-licensed firmware.
Firmware {{VERSION}} for the Elecrow CrowPanel ESP32 Display 7.0" (DIS08070H, board V3.0). Needs Roon and the TextFromTrack companion. Pictures from the panel's showcase mode: the song, lyrics and cover are invented.
