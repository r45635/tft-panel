Install TFT Panel

Firmware v0.1.18 for the Elecrow CrowPanel 7" (DIS08070H, board V3.0) and the Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Touch-LCD-7B. The right version is picked for the screen you connect.

Only these two screens.
The installer recognises the chip, not the screen: any other ESP32-P4 board (for example the Guition JC1060P470C) would get the Waveshare firmware and its display would stay dark. Other boards are not supported yet — see other boards.
  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS, Linux). Phones and Safari/Firefox cannot do this.
  2. Connect the panel's USB-C port to the computer with a cable that carries data.
  3. Click Install, pick the serial port and confirm. It takes about 3 minutes on the CrowPanel, about 1 minute on the Waveshare.
    • CrowPanel: USB Serial / CH340 / usbserial
    • Waveshare: use the USB-C port USB 1.1 (not USB to UART); the port is USB JTAG/serial debug unit / usbmodem
  4. The panel restarts on its Wi-Fi setup screen: scan the first QR code with your phone to join it, then the second one to choose your Wi-Fi network.
This browser cannot flash devices: please open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Flashing needs a secure page (https).
No serial port in the list?
CrowPanel on Windows: install the CH340 USB driver from WCH, then unplug and replug the panel (macOS and Linux already include it). The Waveshare needs no driver. Some USB-C cables only charge: try another one.
After this, no cable needed.
The panel checks for new versions by itself and shows Update at the top of the screen: tap it to install. If an update ever fails to start, the panel goes back to the previous version. The user guide explains the rest.

TFT Panel is open source (MIT): github.com/r45635/tft-panel. It shows synced lyrics from your own TextFromTrack companion on your local network.
