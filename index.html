A remote you never have to unlock

A 7-inch touch screen that controls what you play on your music server and shows it — the cover, the lyrics in time with the song, your library, the zones and the volume. Give it your Wi-Fi and it finds your music server by itself: no computer left running, no app to launch, no account.

Install in your browser Read the user guide
Nothing else to runThe panel talks to your music server itself. Your Wi-Fi is the only thing it needs to be told.
Touch controlsPlay, pause, skip, a volume slider, mute, and the choice of the zone to follow.
Your whole librarySearch it, walk through artists and albums, start one on the zone you are listening to.
Synced lyricsThe current line large, the next ones gently sliding up, timed to a few hundredths of a second.
Cover viewSwipe for the album art with the title and artist in large type. Songs without lyrics show it by themselves.
A clock when the music stopsThe time, the weather of your town and the day's headlines, then the screen dims and sleeps.
Set up from your phoneTwo QR codes on the screen: join the panel, pick your Wi-Fi. No app, no account.
Updates over the airA tap installs a new version; if it ever fails to start, the panel goes back by itself.
Private and openTalks only to machines on your own network. MIT-licensed firmware, three boards, one image.

Where the lyrics come from

Roon serves none of its own, so the words are the one thing the panel is told where to find. Run the TextFromTrack companion anywhere on your network — one container, nothing to enable in Roon, and the panel finds it by itself. Mount your music folder in it and the lyrics become yours: the .lrc files beside your files, the words in your tags, the wrong ones replaced. Would you rather run nothing? Point the panel straight at lrclib.net or at your own mirror of it.

Firmware v0.1.23 for the Elecrow CrowPanel ESP32 Display 7.0" (DIS08070H, board V3.0), the Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Touch-LCD-7B and the VIEWE UEP4S070H1024V600C-WB-C. Needs Roon on your network; the TextFromTrack companion is optional. Pictures from the panel's showcase mode: the song, lyrics and cover are invented.
